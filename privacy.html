Effective Date: January 2025

At BotAnex Forge, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal information that is collected, how it is used, and the steps we take to safeguard it. By using our services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

We collect the following types of personal information when you use our services:

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. These third parties are obligated to protect your data and only use it as necessary to provide their services.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can request access to the personal data we hold about you.

  • Deletion: You can request that we delete your personal data, subject to certain exceptions.

Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies are small data files that are stored on your device and are used to track usage patterns and preferences. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features of the site may not function properly without them.
